# Modernism 

## Definition
Modernism is a broad trend that shows rejection of the past and embrace the brave and rational future 
in the cire ideas of art, literature, design, and architecture and music. 

## History and Origins.
A broadly defined multinational cultural movement that grew out of the philosophical, scientific, 
political, and ideological shift that followed the Industrial Revolution. It was the re-evaluation 
of the assumptions and aesthetic values of their predecessors. Modernism began developing in the late 
19th century and became especially influential through the 20th century. Industrialism, urbanization, 
and other social chances encouraged artists and designers to rethink traditional ways of creating art and design. 

## Main Characteristics

Some of the main characteristics of Modernism include: 

-Experimentation and Innovation 
-Rejection of Traditional Realism
-Fragmentation and Collage
-Symbolism and Allusion
-Universalism
-Scalability 
-Reorder
